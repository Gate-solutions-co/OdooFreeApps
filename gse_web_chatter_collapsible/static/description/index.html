Overview

Standard Odoo vs. GSE Collapsible Chatter

The Challenge in Standard Odoo

  • Side chatter permanently consumes 35% to 40% of your screen width.
  • Notebook tabs (e.g., in Employees, Sales, CRM) overflow into ugly horizontal scrollbars.
  • Large one2many tables and fields become cramped and require constant horizontal panning.
  • No native ability to temporarily hide or collapse the chatter on demand.

The Gate Solutions Way

  • 1-Click Collapse: The form sheet smoothly expands to 100% full width.
  • All notebook tabs and detailed tables fit naturally with zero horizontal scrolling.
  • Minimalist, sleek floating handle showing only the arrow and chat icon.
  • Freely Draggable: Move the handle up or down along the edge to match your screen layout.
Features

Designed for Speed, Comfort & Productivity

Everything is automated and plug-and-play with zero configuration needed.

1-Click Collapse & Expand

A clean collapse arrow inside the chatter top bar lets you collapse notes and messages with a single click whenever you need more workspace.

Draggable Up & Down

Easily click and drag the collapsed tab up or down to place it at the exact vertical height that suits your screen, staying cleanly out of your way.

100% Full-Width Form

When collapsed, the form sheet expands smoothly to 100% width, immediately eliminating horizontal scrolling on notebook tabs and wide tables.

Smart Local Persistence

Remembers your preference (open or closed) and your chosen vertical drag height in your browser, maintaining your preferred layout across records.

Action Auto-Open & Shortcuts

Triggering mail actions or using keyboard shortcuts (m for Send message, Shift+M for Log note, Shift+A for Activity) automatically expands the chatter.

Full RTL & Arabic Support

Seamless bidirectional layout. Automatically adapts chevron directions and edge anchors when switching between English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL).

Zero Configuration Required

Simply install the module and it immediately works across all standard and custom Odoo form views (Employees, Sales, CRM, Invoicing, Inventory, Purchase, Projects, etc.). No extra settings or technical knowledge required.
